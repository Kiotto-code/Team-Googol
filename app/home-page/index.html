How FINDR. Works

Three simple steps to recover your lost items

1

Found an Item?

Scan the QR code on any FINDR box, take a picture in the web app, and securely place the item inside the box.

2

Lost Something?

Search with a simple description in our web app. Our AI instantly matches your query to items in FINDR boxes.

3

Claim Your Item

If matched, simply scan your RFID card at the box to unlock it and retrieve your item securely.
